Twenty years of testing and I still got humbled by a two-letter typo.

geo-kids pulls every flag straight from a country code — "JP" gets you Japan's flag, "FR" gets you France's, automatically. Clean, simple, works great.

Until it doesn't. Get one code wrong and the app doesn't crash. It doesn't throw an error. It just quietly shows the wrong flag, confident as anything, and looks completely normal doing it.

That's the bug I actually respect. Not the loud one that stops the app — the silent one that just lies to you politely. No stack trace, no red screen, nothing to grep for. You only catch it by actually looking, country by country, and asking "wait, is that really Seychelles?"

This is most of what testing actually is, honestly. Not "did it crash" — "did it tell the truth."

My daughter caught two of these before I did. She just knows what a Japanese flag looks like.
