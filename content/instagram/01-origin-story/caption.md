My kid asked me where Kiribati is and I had no idea. That's basically how geo-kids started.

I've spent my whole career testing software — mostly the manual, exploratory kind, the kind where you poke at things until they break. So when my daughter got obsessed with flags and capitals, I didn't buy an app. I built one.

No framework, no build step, just vanilla JS/HTML/CSS and a Hebrew-first interface because that's the language she plays in. Right now it covers all 195 UN-recognized countries, each one with a capital, a population, a fun fact, and a landmark — written in Hebrew that's actually warm and kid-friendly, not a stiff translation.

It's slow going. It's also the most fun I've had coding in years, because the QA is a six-year-old yelling "that flag is WRONG" from the couch, and she's usually right.
