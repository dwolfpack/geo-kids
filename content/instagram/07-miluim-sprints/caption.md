Try explaining "sprint planning" to a reservist call-up sometime.

Working in Israeli tech means your team's capacity can change overnight, for reasons that have nothing to do with the roadmap. Miluim doesn't check your Jira board first. Someone's phone rings, and a week later your two-person feature team is a one-person feature team, indefinitely, and the sprint commitment you made on Monday is now a polite fiction.

You adjust. Everyone here has learned to adjust — quietly, without much drama, because complaining about it doesn't get the work done and everyone's in the same boat eventually. It's just part of the texture of building software in this country right now: informal, intense, occasionally interrupted by something much bigger than a deadline, and somehow still shipping.

geo-kids doesn't have sprints or stakeholders. It has whatever hour I get after my kids are asleep. Some weeks that's a lot. Some weeks it's one bug fix and a Hebrew typo caught at 11pm. Either way, it moves.
