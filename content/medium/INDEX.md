# Medium Article Set — Index

Five long-form essays, each in its own numbered folder with an `article.md` and an `images/` folder containing two SVG diagrams.

1. **[What 195 Countries Taught Me About Regression Testing](01-regression-testing-195-countries/article.md)**
   Expanding a kids' geography app from 100 to 195 countries by hand becomes a real-world lesson in why regression testing and data-integrity discipline matter at any scale — and why "silently wrong" is more dangerous than "broken."

2. **[Monolith vs. Microservices: What Actually Changes When You Test Them](02-monolith-vs-microservices/article.md)**
   An honest, experience-grounded comparison of how testing strategy, ownership, and failure modes shift when a system splits apart — from total-but-obvious monolith failures to hidden, seam-level microservices bugs.

3. **[Keeping Trust When the Process Changes Faster Than People Can Absorb It](03-managing-through-transition/article.md)**
   Lessons from managing a QA team through a re-org, a monolith-to-microservices restructuring, and layoffs — on the gap between announcing a change and people actually absorbing it, and how to keep trust intact.

4. **[The Bug a Script Will Never Find](04-manual-exploratory-testing-craft/article.md)**
   A defense of manual and exploratory testing as a craft, built around a real "silently wrong, not broken" Hebrew-localization bug in the geo-kids country data that no validation script could ever have caught.

5. **[What My Kids Taught Me About Product Thinking](05-parenting-side-projects-israel/article.md)**
   Building a geography game with and for his kids reshaped how the author thinks about product decisions, patience, and staying hands-on after moving into management — set against the backdrop of working in Israel's tech industry through a hard stretch.
