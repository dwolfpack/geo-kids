## The offer

A fixed-fee **AI readiness audit** for founder-led businesses: 2-3 weeks,
$2,500, four deliverables — an AI readiness scorecard, a tool-stack audit, a
90-day roadmap, and a 90-minute walkthrough with the leadership team. The fee is
credited against implementation work if they hire you to execute the roadmap.

That credit clause is the actual closer. It converts the audit from a cost into
a deposit, and it is why you can charge at the bottom of the published $2-8k band
without looking cheap.

## Why you and not anyone else

**Incomplete — blocked on your domain.** The structural fit is excellent: an
audit is pure domain judgement plus selling, with zero building required of you.
But "AI readiness audit" sold by a generalist is a commodity, and the category is
crowded with consultancies publishing the same four deliverables.

The version that works is *"the AI readiness audit for [your industry], by
someone who has run [your function] in it for N years."* That sentence is the
entire moat. Fill in `profile.operator.domains` and this section writes itself.

## The buyer

Founder-led businesses, roughly NIS 5-50M revenue, 10-80 employees, in your
domain. The qualifying signal is a founder who personally feels an expensive
manual workflow and has no CTO to ask about it.

Reachable through: your existing network first (this is the whole reason the play
works), industry association events and mailing lists, and a free 30-minute "AI
waste review" as the top of funnel.

Not reachable through: cold email, LinkedIn spray, or ads. Do not try — the
credibility you are selling does not survive cold outreach.

## Pricing

- **$2,500** fixed fee, SMB scope — bottom of the published $2-8k band
- **Credited in full** against a subsequent implementation engagement
- Implementation retainer afterwards: $2,000-5,000/month

Why they say yes: they are already spending more than $2,500/month on the
inefficiency, and every AI vendor pitching them is selling a product while you
are selling a decision. Pertama Partners publishes $15k-120k for fixed-scope
assessments at the mid-market tier; you are visibly a bargain at SMB scope.

## First five customers

1. **Pick one vertical inside your domain and write down 15 named businesses.**
   Not a category — 15 names. If you cannot list 15, the vertical is wrong or you
   do not have the access you think you have.
2. **Offer 3 free "mini-audits"** to the three warmest: 90-minute conversation,
   plus a 3-page memo produced by the agent within 48 hours. Free, no pitch.
3. **Convert the mini-audits into paid full audits.** The memo does the selling;
   it demonstrates that you see things they did not. Expect roughly 1 of 3.
4. **Ask every mini-audit recipient for two referrals**, whether they buy or not.
   This is the compounding step and the one most people skip.
5. **Run the first paid audit, and write the case study before the invoice is
   paid** — while they are still enthusiastic. That case study is the asset that
   makes customers 4 and 5 cheap.

## What the agent builds

1. Audit questionnaire and scoring framework, tuned to the chosen vertical
2. Deliverable templates: scorecard, stack audit, 90-day roadmap (`docx`/`pdf` skills)
3. The 3-page mini-audit memo template, and each filled instance from your notes
4. A one-page offer sheet and proposal template (Canva)
5. Per-client filled deliverables from your interview notes
6. A simple pipeline tracker as a Google Sheet — not a CRM

Blocked on you: nothing. No account, no credential, no payment required.

## What you personally do

~7 hrs/week: 3h outreach and referral asks, 2.5h client conversations, 1.5h
reviewing and correcting agent-drafted deliverables. **Zero build hours** — if

## Kill criteria

Written now, while they are cheap to accept:

- **Fewer than 3 of 15 named businesses agree to a free 90-minute conversation
  by day 10** → your access to this vertical is weaker than assumed. Kill, re-pick.
- **Three mini-audits produce zero paid conversions** → the offer or the price is
  wrong. One more iteration on the offer, then kill.
- **You are past 10 hrs/week by day 14** → the scoping is wrong; it will not
  survive alongside your job.

## Costs and risks

- **Cost: $0.** No tools, no ads, no accounts beyond what you have.
- **Reputational:** you are staking your name on the recommendations. Mitigation:
  you review every deliverable, and the roadmap recommends nothing you cannot
  personally defend.
- **Commodity pressure:** the category is crowded. Mitigation is the vertical,
  which is exactly the blocked section above.
- **Not tax or legal advice.** Invoicing, VAT, and business-entity questions are
  yours and your accountant's.
