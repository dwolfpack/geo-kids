## The offer

Set up a **Hebrew-speaking WhatsApp AI agent** for Israeli service SMBs —
handling inbound enquiries, scheduling, quotes, FAQs, and lead capture, with a
clean handoff to a human when it should not answer.

- **₪8,000 one-time** setup
- **₪1,500/month** managed retainer (monitoring, prompt tuning, monthly report)

## Why you and not anyone else

Three things stack here, and the combination is genuinely hard to copy:

1. **Hebrew.** Remote competitors cannot deliver a bot that handles Hebrew
   customer conversations with the right register. This is a real language moat,
   not a nice-to-have.
2. **Local trust.** Israeli SMB owners buy from people they or their peers know.
   Your distribution ability is the scarce input, and it is not purchasable.
3. **WhatsApp is the channel.** ~99% of Israelis use WhatsApp as the de facto
   business communication layer, so you are automating the channel they already
   live in rather than asking them to adopt anything new.

**What is still missing:** the vertical. "Israeli SMBs" is not a buyer. Pick one
where you have real access.

## The buyer

Israeli service businesses with high inbound WhatsApp volume and nobody to answer
it. Candidate verticals: dental and aesthetic clinics, veterinary clinics, driving
schools, moving companies, event venues, garages, private tutoring networks.

The qualifying question is one sentence: *"How many WhatsApp messages do you miss
after 18:00?"* If the answer is "a lot", they are a buyer. If they do not know,
they are a better buyer.

Reachable through: warm intros in your network, Israeli small-business Facebook
and WhatsApp groups, trade associations, and local business development centres.

## Pricing

- Setup **₪8,000**, sits in the middle of the published ₪5,000-15,000 band for
  no-code configured setups
- Retainer **₪1,500/mo**, at the low end of the published ₪800-2,000 band
- Custom builds in this market are published at ₪15,000-40,000 — your positioning
  is deliberately below that

Why they say yes: a receptionist costs ₪8,000-10,000/month fully loaded. You are
not competing with that — you are selling the recovery of leads they currently
lose overnight. One recovered dental implant consultation pays for the setup.

## First five customers

1. **Pick one vertical and list 20 named businesses in it.** One vertical only —
   the second bot in the same vertical costs a fraction of the first, and that
   margin is the entire business.
2. **Get warm intros to 5.** Ask for a 20-minute diagnostic call, not a sale.
3. **Before the second call, have the agent build a working demo using their own
   FAQ and their own tone.** This is the closer. Do not pitch a concept — show
   them their own bot answering their own customers' questions.
4. **Offer the first two as "founding clients" at ₪4,000** in exchange for a
   testimonial and permission to use them as a named case study. Discount the
   price, never the retainer.
5. **Use the case study on the remaining 15.** In a tight vertical, the second
   sale is dramatically easier than the first, and the fifteenth is nearly
   automatic.

## What the agent builds

1. A working Hebrew demo bot per prospect, from their real public FAQ (step 3 above)
2. Hebrew conversation flows: booking, pricing enquiry, hours, location, escalation
3. Handoff rules — explicitly including what the bot must refuse to answer
4. Client-facing one-pager and pricing sheet (Canva)
5. A monthly report template (messages handled, leads captured, escalations)
6. An onboarding checklist so client #3 onwards takes hours, not days

**Blocked on you or the client:**

- **Meta Business verification** for each client's WhatsApp Business API access.
  This is real friction, takes days, and the client must do it. Raise it on the
  first call, not the fifth — surprises here kill deals.
- WhatsApp per-message fees: the client's cost, on their own account. Never yours.

## Kill criteria

- **Fewer than 3 of 5 warm intros take a diagnostic call by day 10** → the
  vertical or the framing is wrong. Re-pick the vertical once, then kill.
- **Neither of the first two personalised demos converts by day 14** → the demo is
  not compelling or the price is wrong. One iteration, then kill.
- **Meta verification blocks both founding clients past day 25** → the friction
  is the business model's problem, not a client problem. Kill and reconsider.
- **Any month where the retainer generates more than 2 hours of your own
  support time per client** → the margin is gone; re-scope or exit.

## Costs and risks

- **Cost: ₪0** to start. Clients pay their own WhatsApp fees on their own accounts.
- **Platform dependency:** Meta controls the API, its pricing, and its policies.
  This is the main structural risk and the reason this scored risk 2 rather than 1.
- **Verification friction** is the most likely practical killer, ahead of sales.
- **Quality exposure:** a bot that mishandles a real customer in Hebrew damages
  your client's reputation and therefore yours. Mitigation: the refusal rules in
  build step 3 are not optional, and you review every flow before go-live.
